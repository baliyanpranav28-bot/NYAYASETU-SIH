# 🏛️ NYAYASETU — Unified Welfare Access Platform

> **One platform. Multiple schemes. Clear eligibility. Better access to welfare.**

NYAYASETU is a unified welfare access platform designed to help citizens discover relevant government welfare schemes, understand their eligibility, identify required documents, and track their applications through a simple and user-friendly interface.

The project is developed as a prototype for **Smart India Hackathon (SIH)** by **Team AuditMinds**.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Citizens often struggle to discover government welfare schemes that they are eligible for.

The major challenges include:

- Lack of awareness about available welfare schemes
- Difficulty understanding eligibility criteria
- Unclear information about required documents
- Multiple government portals and scattered information
- Difficulty tracking applications
- Risk of missing benefits for which a citizen may be eligible

NYAYASETU aims to simplify this process through a single unified platform.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

NYAYASETU acts as a bridge between citizens and government welfare schemes.

Instead of searching through multiple portals, a citizen can provide basic information about themselves and receive relevant welfare schemes based on their profile.

The platform provides:

**Citizen Profile → Scheme Discovery → Eligibility → Documents → Application → Tracking**

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👤 Citizen Profile
Users can provide basic information about themselves to help identify relevant welfare schemes.

### 🔍 Find My Schemes
The platform helps citizens discover welfare schemes relevant to their profile.

### ✅ Eligibility Understanding
Citizens can easily understand why they may or may not be eligible for a particular scheme.

### 📄 Document Requirements
The platform helps users understand which documents are required for applying.

### 📋 Scheme Details
Users can view important information about schemes, including eligibility and benefits.

### 📊 Application Tracking
Citizens can track the progress/status of their applications.

### ⚠️ Problem Reporting
Users can report problems or issues related to their welfare application.

### 🏛️ Government-side View
The prototype also demonstrates a government-side interface for viewing and processing applications.

### 🔄 Benefit Overlap Detection
The system demonstrates identification of possible overlapping benefits or schemes.
